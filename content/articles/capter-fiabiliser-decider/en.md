Three disciplines, one loop: automation to capture, development to refine, artificial intelligence to decide. Every decision goes back to the business teams.

> **In brief**: capture signals from production and support services, make the data reliable, turn it into decisions. AI detects and predicts, human expertise arbitrates.

:::figure signaux "The loop: three steps, and every decision goes back to the business teams."

## Capture: make every machine and every department speak

Sensors, PLCs, industrial networks and support-service systems: the aim is to make every machine and every department speak.

This step belongs to automation. The related projects are gathered under [Automation projects](/en/projects.html#automation).

## Refine: cleanse, standardise, rationalise

Cleanse, standardise, rationalise: the aim is a single source of truth and clear tools that make data usable.

This step belongs to development. The related projects are gathered under [Applications and industrial transformation](/en/projects.html#applications).

## Decide: people and AI, together

AI detects and predicts, human expertise arbitrates: together they turn data into performance. Every decision goes back to the business teams, which closes the loop.

Two projects to look at for this step:

- [See it before it breaks](/en/projects/downtime-risk-monitoring.html): a machine-fleet dashboard that goes beyond status displays to help anticipate downtime and prioritise action.
- [Orélia](/en/projects/local-ai-assistant.html): a personal AI assistant that runs 100% locally, where a conductor delegates to specialised agents, with guardrails on files. Personal project.
