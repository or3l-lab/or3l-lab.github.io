02The challenge

On these machines, the applicator head must follow the product speed, apply, cut and return, within a few hundred milliseconds, without ever losing synchronisation.

During my time at Cypack, some machines reached one product every 200 ms, or 300 products per minute. The simulation below illustrates handle application using indicative values.

03What I did

  • Programmed the axis synchronisation (electronic cam).
  • Configured drives and servomotors.
  • On-site tuning on customers' lines.
  • Trained operators and maintenance teams.

04How it works

05Adhesive-tape bundling

Another special-machinery application: automatically grouping several products into a bundle held by adhesive tape. This is separate from handle application and likewise calls for synchronisation, format adaptation and commissioning.
