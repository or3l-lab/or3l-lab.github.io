02The challenge

You cannot switch everything at once on a tool production depends on: the platform had to be modernised without taking risks.

03What I did

  • Inventory and baseline of current behaviour.
  • Full preparation outside production.
  • Switch-over in waves, with automated checks.
  • Rollback tested before D-day.
