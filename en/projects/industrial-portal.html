02The challenge

Over time, tools multiply, each with its own accounts, rules and ergonomics. The goal: simpler access, reliable rights and lighter maintenance.

03What I did

  • Architecture design: a common core and business modules.
  • Role-based access rights, systematically enforced server-side.
  • Consistent, accessible, mobile-friendly interface.
  • Profile-based acceptance testing before every release.
