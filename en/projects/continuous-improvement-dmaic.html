02The challenge

Short stops were eroding a line's performance. Everyone had an explanation; nobody had the data.

03What I did

  • Problem and measurable target defined and shared with the team.
  • Shop-floor measurement of stops and cycle-time variation.
  • Pareto and 5 Whys to isolate root causes.
  • Solutions trialled in small steps (PDCA), then standardised.
  • Long-term control: visual indicators, 5S and regular checkpoints.
