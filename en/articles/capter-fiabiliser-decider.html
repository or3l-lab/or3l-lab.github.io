01Introduction

Three disciplines, one loop: automation to capture, development to refine, artificial intelligence to decide. Every decision goes back to the business teams.

In brief: capture signals from production and support services, make the data reliable, turn it into decisions. AI detects and predicts, human expertise arbitrates.

02Capture: make every machine and every department speak

Sensors, PLCs, industrial networks and support-service systems: the aim is to make every machine and every department speak.

This step belongs to automation. The related projects are gathered under Automation projects.

03Refine: cleanse, standardise, rationalise

Cleanse, standardise, rationalise: the aim is a single source of truth and clear tools that make data usable.

This step belongs to development. The related projects are gathered under Applications and industrial transformation.

A project to look at for this step: One reliable dataset to steer the factory, a centralised Power BI report that brings the data together, checks it and refreshes it automatically.

04Decide: people and AI, together

AI detects and predicts, human expertise arbitrates: together they turn data into performance. Every decision goes back to the business teams, which closes the loop.

Two projects to look at for this step:

  • See it before it breaks: a machine-fleet dashboard that goes beyond status displays to help anticipate downtime and prioritise action.
  • Orélia: a personal AI assistant that runs 100% locally, where a conductor delegates to specialised agents, with guardrails on files. Personal project.
